Sketchpad's TX-2 display service drawing on the oscilloscope

This page shows a picture drawn by original 1962-63 Sketchpad code (the sequence-60 oscilloscope display service SQ60A...SQ60FEW from the LMH Y3HT listing, CHM cat. 102726903 vol. 2, transcribed in TX-2/Sketchpad sk2.tx2as lines 5376-5512). The code runs unaltered on a TX-2 simulator; every dot below is one real TSD display transfer to unit 60 (Users Handbook unit sheet "No. 60 OSCILLOSCOPE DISPLAY", word format per Lincoln Lab TR-296 Fig. 4.5). Each of the 10 spots is intensified 8 times per pass - the original's fewer-than-50-point brightening loop.

Scope face (1024x1024 raster, origin at centre)

10 distinct beam points from 80 display events - square + centre dot supplied as display-file words (harness input); streaming, repetition and exit are the original code's. Raw files: scope_view.svg, scope_trace.jsonl.
